# Master Services Agreement (excerpt)

## Termination

Either party may **terminate** this agreement with ninety (90) days written notice.
Material breach allows immediate termination if uncured within thirty (30) days.

## Indemnification

**Indemnification clause:** Vendor shall defend and hold Customer harmless against third-party claims
arising from infringement of intellectual property, except where Customer modifies the deliverables.

## Liability cap

Aggregate liability is limited to fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.
