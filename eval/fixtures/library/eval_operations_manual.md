# Operations Manual — Field Edition

## Safety section

**Safety section requirements:**
- Wear ANSI-rated eye protection when operating the calibration laser.
- Maintain 1.5 m clearance around the thermal exhaust port during burn-in.
- Emergency stop must be tested at the start of each shift.
