# Atmospheric Optics — Rayleigh Scattering Primer

## Abstract

This paper explains **Rayleigh scattering** and why shorter wavelengths dominate clear-sky coloration.

## Rayleigh scattering explanation

Rayleigh scattering occurs when particle diameter is much smaller than the wavelength of light.
Blue light (shorter wavelength) scatters more strongly than red, making the daytime sky appear blue.
