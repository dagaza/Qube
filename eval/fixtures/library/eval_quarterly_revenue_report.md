# Quarterly Revenue Report — Q3 FY2026

## Executive summary

Consolidated revenue for the third quarter reached **$4.2 million**, up 8% quarter-over-quarter.
Operating margin improved to 19% after cost controls in cloud infrastructure.

## Main risks

1. **Customer concentration** — two enterprise accounts represent 34% of ARR.
2. **FX exposure** — EUR-denominated contracts may compress margin if USD strengthens.
3. **Supply-chain delays** for on-prem appliance shipments could slip Q4 recognition.
4. **Regulatory review** in two EU markets may slow new logo acquisition.

This PDF summary is intended for board review. Confidentiality applies to all figures until public earnings release.
