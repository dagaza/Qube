Why birds take dust baths Birds take dust baths to remove parasites and excess oil from their feathers. Dust bathing is common in sparrows, quail, and many other species.
Dust bathing behavior in birds Ornithologists observe that dust baths help birds maintain feather condition and reduce parasite load in dry environments.
Garden bird care tips Provide a dry sandy patch in your garden so local birds can dust-bathe safely away from predators.
