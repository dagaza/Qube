# Project Osprey — Meeting Notes

Project Osprey installs temperature and humidity sensors in the three distribution warehouses and connects them to the facilities dashboard.

## Kick-off — 15 January 2026

Attendees: Marcus (facilities), Aiko (IT infrastructure), Dev (procurement), Sofia (operations).

- Scope: 240 wireless sensors across the Northfield, Riverside and Eastgate warehouses, gateways, and a dashboard with alerts.
- Target go-live: 30 April 2026, before the summer peak.
- Budget: £96,000, of which £58,000 is hardware.

Actions: Dev to run the sensor tender; Aiko to confirm network capacity for the gateways.

## Weekly sync — 29 January 2026

- Three suppliers responded to the tender. Two meet the battery-life requirement of five years.
- Aiko found that Riverside's network cannot take the gateways without a switch upgrade (about £4,000).
- Decision: approve the switch upgrade from the contingency line.

## Steering meeting — 12 February 2026

Decision: the supplier contract is awarded to the lower-cost bidder that meets the five-year battery requirement. Go-live stays at 30 April. Northfield goes first as the pilot, from 2 March.

- Alert thresholds agreed: temperature outside 8–25 °C or relative humidity above 70 % for more than 15 minutes.

## Weekly sync — 26 March 2026

- Northfield pilot live with 80 sensors. Two sensors dropped offline because of metal shelving; moved to the end of the aisles.
- False alerts in the first week came from loading-bay doors left open; the bay zones now use a 30-minute delay.
- Riverside installation starts 6 April; Eastgate on 20 April.

Actions: Sofia to write the alert response procedure for night shifts.

## Go-live review — 30 April 2026

All three warehouses live with 236 working sensors (4 faulty units returned under warranty). The dashboard is in daily use by operations. Project closed under budget at £91,500.
