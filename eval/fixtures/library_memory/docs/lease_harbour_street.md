# Residential Tenancy Agreement — Flat 3B, 14 Harbour Street

This agreement is made between Brightwater Lettings Ltd ("the Landlord") and the tenant named in Schedule 1 ("the Tenant") for the furnished one-bedroom flat at Flat 3B, 14 Harbour Street ("the Property").

## 1. Term

The tenancy is a fixed term of twelve months, starting on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027. After the fixed term it continues month to month on the same terms unless either party gives notice under clause 9.

## 2. Rent

2.1 The rent is £1,450 per calendar month, payable in advance on the first day of each month by standing order.

2.2 If rent is more than five days late, the Landlord may charge a late-payment fee of 3 % of the overdue amount, plus interest at 3 % above the Bank of England base rate from the due date.

2.3 The Landlord may review the rent once a year, on the anniversary of the start date, with at least two months' written notice. Any increase is capped at the Consumer Prices Index plus 1 %.

## 3. Deposit

3.1 The Tenant pays a deposit of £1,670, equal to five weeks' rent, on signing.

3.2 The deposit is protected in a government-approved tenancy deposit scheme within 30 days of receipt. The Landlord will give the Tenant the scheme's details and certificate.

3.3 At the end of the tenancy the deposit is returned within ten days of the parties agreeing any deductions. Deductions may be made only for unpaid rent, damage beyond fair wear and tear, missing inventory items and cleaning needed to return the Property to its check-in condition.

## 4. Utilities and council tax

The Tenant is responsible for electricity, gas, water, broadband and council tax from the start date. The Landlord pays the building service charge and the buildings insurance.

## 5. Use of the property

5.1 The Property may be used only as a private home for the Tenant and no more than one other permanent occupant.

5.2 The Tenant may not sublet the Property, or any part of it, or take in lodgers, without the Landlord's written consent.

5.3 Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere inside the Property or on the balcony.

## 6. Pets

6.1 The Tenant may keep one cat or one small caged animal. No dogs are allowed without separate written consent, which the Landlord will not unreasonably refuse.

6.2 Where a pet is kept, the Tenant must have the carpets professionally cleaned at the end of the tenancy and provide the receipt. No additional pet deposit is charged.

## 7. Repairs and access

7.1 The Landlord keeps the structure, exterior, heating, hot water, plumbing and electrical installations in repair.

7.2 The Tenant reports repairs promptly through the Landlord's online portal. Emergencies (flooding, no heating in winter, a gas smell, a security failure) are reported by phone at any time.

7.3 The Landlord may enter the Property for inspections and repairs after giving at least 24 hours' written notice, at reasonable times of day, except in an emergency.

7.4 The Tenant must not redecorate, drill into tiled surfaces or change the locks without written consent.

## 8. Inventory

An inventory and schedule of condition, with photographs, is attached as Schedule 2. The Tenant has seven days from the start date to return it with any comments.

## 9. Ending the tenancy

9.1 During the fixed term neither party may end the tenancy early, except under clause 9.3.

9.2 After the fixed term, the Tenant may end the tenancy by giving at least one month's written notice, ending on the day before a rent day. The Landlord must give at least two months' written notice.

9.3 Break clause: either party may end the tenancy after the sixth month by giving two months' written notice, so that the earliest end date is 31 August 2026.

9.4 At check-out the Tenant returns all keys (two front-door keys, one mailbox key and one bike-store fob).

## 10. Renewal

If both parties wish to renew for a further fixed term, the Landlord will send a renewal offer at least three months before the end of the fixed term. A renewal fee is not charged.
