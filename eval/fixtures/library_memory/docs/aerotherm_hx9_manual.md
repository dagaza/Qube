# Aerotherm HX-9 Air-to-Water Heat Pump — Owner's Manual

Revision 3. This manual covers the indoor control unit and the outdoor unit of the Aerotherm HX-9. Keep it near the indoor unit for the life of the product.

## 1. Safety

Read this section before operating the system. Installation, refrigerant work and electrical connection must be carried out by a certified installer. Do not open the outdoor unit casing while the system is powered; the fan can start without warning when the defrost cycle ends.

Keep at least 50 cm of clear space in front of the indoor unit and 30 cm around the outdoor unit. Never cover the outdoor unit with a tarpaulin in winter: it needs free airflow to defrost. Children must not play with the unit or the controller.

If you smell burning or hear grinding from the compressor, switch the system off at the isolator and contact your installer.

## 3. The controller

The indoor controller has a 3.5-inch display, four buttons (Mode, Up, Down, OK) and a status LED. The LED is green when the system runs normally, amber during defrost and red when an error code is shown.

Press and hold OK for three seconds to unlock the buttons. The controller locks itself again after two minutes without input.

## 4. Operating modes

**Heat.** The standard mode. The system follows the weather-compensation curve set by your installer: the colder it is outside, the warmer the water sent to the radiators or underfloor loops.

**Cool.** Available only with underfloor or fan-coil emitters. The flow temperature never drops below 18 °C, to avoid condensation on floors.

**Eco.** Lowers the target room temperature by 2 °C and caps the compressor at 70 % of its maximum speed. Use Eco at night or when the home is empty for less than three days.

**Boost.** Raises the domestic hot water cylinder to 60 °C once, using the immersion heater as well as the compressor. Boost switches itself off when the cylinder reaches temperature or after 90 minutes, whichever comes first.

**Holiday.** Holds the rooms at 10 °C for frost protection and skips hot water heating until the return date you enter.

## 5. Domestic hot water

The default cylinder temperature is 48 °C. Once a week, on Sunday at 02:00, the system runs an anti-legionella cycle that heats the cylinder to 60 °C and holds it for 30 minutes. You can move the cycle to another day in Settings → Hot water → Legionella, but you cannot switch it off from the controller.

## 6. Defrost

In cold, damp weather ice builds up on the outdoor coil. The unit detects this and reverses the refrigerant flow for two to six minutes to melt the ice. During defrost the outdoor fan stops, the status LED turns amber and you may see steam rising from the unit. This is normal. Heating output resumes automatically.

## 7. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Low refrigerant pressure | Switch off and call your installer. Do not reset repeatedly. |
| E2 | High discharge temperature | Check that radiator valves are open; reset once. |
| E3 | Water flow too low | Bleed the radiators and check the system pressure (1.0–2.0 bar). |
| E4 | Outdoor temperature sensor fault | Call your installer; the unit runs on a default curve meanwhile. |
| E5 | Communication lost between indoor and outdoor unit | Power-cycle both units at the isolator. |
| E7 | Defrost did not complete | Clear snow or leaves from around the outdoor unit, then reset. |
| E9 | Immersion heater overheat cut-out | Do not reset; call your installer. |

To reset an error, hold Mode and OK together for five seconds. If the same code returns within 24 hours, do not reset again.

## 8. Routine maintenance

Clean the magnetic filter on the return pipe every three months: close the two isolating valves, unscrew the cap, rinse the magnet under the tap and refit. Check the system pressure gauge monthly; top up through the filling loop if it falls below 1.0 bar.

Keep the outdoor unit clear of leaves, snow and garden furniture. Rinse the outdoor coil gently with a hose once a year, in spring. Never use a pressure washer, which bends the fins.

## 9. Annual service

Have the system serviced once a year by a certified installer. The service includes a refrigerant leak check, electrical safety checks, a check of the expansion vessel charge (1.0 bar when empty), cleaning of the condensate drain and a software update of the controller. Record each service in the log at the back of this manual.

## 10. Warranty

The Aerotherm HX-9 carries a seven-year parts warranty and a two-year labour warranty, provided that:

- the product is registered within 30 days of installation;
- it was installed by a certified installer;
- it receives an annual service, recorded in the service log.

The warranty does not cover damage from frost when the system was switched off at the isolator, from incorrect water treatment, or from blocked airflow around the outdoor unit. To make a claim, contact the installer first; they will need the serial number on the label inside the controller door.
