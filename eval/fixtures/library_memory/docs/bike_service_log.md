# Gravel Bike — Service Log and Maintenance Plan

Frame: steel gravel frame, size 56. Groupset: 1×11 with hydraulic disc brakes. Wheels: 700c tubeless, 40 mm tyres.

## Maintenance intervals

- Chain: clean and lube every 300 km, or after every wet ride. Measure wear with the checker every 1,000 km; replace at 0.5 % stretch.
- Tyre pressure: 38 psi front, 42 psi rear on gravel; 50 psi both on tarmac. Top up the tubeless sealant every two months (40 ml per tyre).
- Brake pads: check thickness monthly. Replace when the pad material is under 1.5 mm. Organic pads last about 1,500 km in winter.
- Brake bleed: once a year, with mineral oil only.
- Cassette: replace every second chain.
- Headset and bottom bracket: check for play every spring.
