# Kestrel Analytics — Staff Handbook

Version 2026.1. This handbook applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors follow their own agreements.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day with your team. A full-time week is 37.5 hours. Overtime must be agreed in advance with your manager and is taken as time off in lieu within three months.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 26 days of annual leave plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

You may carry over up to five unused days into the next leave year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost. Book leave through the HR system at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. The office closes between Christmas and New Year, and three of your annual leave days are reserved for that closure.

## Sickness absence

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. You can self-certify for up to seven calendar days; after that we need a fit note. Kestrel pays full salary for the first 20 working days of sickness absence in any rolling twelve months and statutory sick pay after that.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of leave at full pay, followed by up to 26 weeks of statutory leave. Partners and adoptive second parents receive 6 weeks at full pay, which can be taken in blocks of one week any time in the first year. Tell HR at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth or placement.

## Remote and hybrid work

Most roles are hybrid: you work from the office at least two days a week, one of which is your team's anchor day. You may work from abroad for up to 20 working days per year, subject to approval from HR, because of tax and insurance rules. Working from a public place is fine for general work but not for calls involving client data.

## Expenses

Claim business expenses through the expenses app within 60 days, with a photo of the receipt for every item over £10. Train travel is standard class unless the journey is over three hours. The daily meal allowance on business trips is £40 in the UK and £55 abroad. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Mileage in your own car is paid at 45p per mile.

## Equipment

You receive a laptop, a monitor and a headset. A one-off home-office allowance of £300 is available in your first three months. Return all equipment on your last day; unreturned equipment is deducted from your final salary.

## Information security

All accounts use multi-factor authentication. Store passwords in the company password manager and never reuse them. Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk. Report suspected phishing with the "Report" button in the mail client; if you clicked a link or entered credentials, call the IT service desk immediately, at any hour.

Client data must stay in approved systems. Never copy client data to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within one hour.

## Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of £1,200 and five study days. Conferences count against the budget. Certification exams are paid for once; a resit is paid by the employee.

## Conduct and grievances

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Raise a concern informally with your manager first. If that does not resolve it, submit a written grievance to HR, who will hold a meeting within ten working days. You may bring a colleague or a trade union representative to any formal meeting.

## Leaving Kestrel

The notice period is one month during probation and three months afterwards, unless your contract says otherwise. Probation lasts six months. Unused annual leave is paid in your final salary.
