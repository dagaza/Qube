# Lisbon Trip — 18 to 23 June 2026

## Travel

Outbound: Thursday 18 June, flight QB 412, departs 07:40, arrives Lisbon 10:25 local time. Checked bag included (23 kg).

Return: Tuesday 23 June, flight QB 419, departs Lisbon 18:55. Be at the airport by 16:45; the metro red line takes about 25 minutes from the city centre.

Lisbon is one hour behind Central European time in summer.

## Accommodation

Casa do Largo, a guesthouse in Alfama, five nights. Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00; bags can be left at reception on the last day. Breakfast included. Booking reference LX-55871. Free cancellation until 4 June.

## Day by day

**Thursday 18 June.** Arrive, drop bags, lunch near the cathedral. Afternoon walk up to the Miradouro de Santa Luzia. Evening: the Santo António festivities are on, so expect crowds and grilled sardines in the streets.

**Friday 19 June.** Tram 28 early in the morning to avoid the queues. Castelo de São Jorge (book tickets online, 10:00 slot). Afternoon in Baixa and Chiado. Dinner booked at Tasca da Esquina Azul at 20:30.

**Saturday 20 June.** Day trip to Sintra by train from Rossio station (about 40 minutes). Pena Palace timed entry at 10:30, then Quinta da Regaleira. Bring walking shoes; the paths are steep.

**Sunday 21 June.** Belém: Jerónimos Monastery (closed Mondays, so Sunday it is), the tower, and the custard tarts. Afternoon at the MAAT museum. Free evening.

**Monday 22 June.** Beach day in Cascais, 40 minutes by train from Cais do Sodré. Return for a fado evening in Alfama, booked for 21:00.

**Tuesday 23 June.** Slow morning, LX Factory market, lunch, then the airport.

## Practical notes

- Load the Viva Viagem card with "zapping" credit; it works on metro, trams, buses and the Sintra and Cascais trains.
- Many small restaurants are cash only.
- Pharmacies are marked with a green cross; one in each district stays open late on a rota.
- Travel insurance: annual multi-trip policy, already active.
