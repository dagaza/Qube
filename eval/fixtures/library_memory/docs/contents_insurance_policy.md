# Tenant Contents Insurance — Policy Wording and Schedule

Insurer: Meridian Mutual. Policy type: contents only, for tenants. Policy period: 12 months from 3 February 2026.

## Schedule

| Cover | Limit |
|---|---|
| Contents sum insured | £25,000 |
| Single article limit | £1,500 |
| Valuables in total | £5,000 |
| Personal possessions away from home | £2,000 (optional cover, included) |
| Accidental damage to contents | Included |
| Tenant's liability for landlord's fixtures | £5,000 |
| Legal expenses | Not included |

Standard excess: £150 per claim. Escape of water excess: £350. Subsidence does not apply to contents-only policies.

## What is covered

We cover your contents against loss or damage caused by fire, smoke, lightning, explosion, storm, flood, escape of water or oil, theft or attempted theft, vandalism, impact, and falling trees or aerials.

Accidental damage cover includes one-off accidents such as spilling paint on a carpet you own or dropping a laptop. It does not include wear and tear, damage by pets, or damage that happens gradually.

## Theft

Theft is covered only if there was forcible and violent entry to, or exit from, the home, or if entry was gained by deception. Theft from an unlocked home, or from a shared area of the building such as a bike store, is not covered unless you have added the bicycle extension.

Bicycles over £500 must be listed individually and kept locked to an immovable object when away from home.

## Making a claim

Report any theft or vandalism to the police within 24 hours and obtain a crime reference number. Tell us about any claim as soon as you can, and in any case within 30 days of the incident. We may ask for receipts, photographs or valuations, and we may send a loss adjuster for claims over £2,500.

We will usually settle by repair or replacement as new, less the excess. Clothing and household linen are settled on an indemnity basis, allowing for wear and tear.

## Exclusions

We do not cover: loss or damage while the home is unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days; damage caused by the landlord's failure to maintain the building; mechanical or electrical breakdown; loss of cash over £250; or any loss caused deliberately by you or a person living with you.

## Cancelling the policy

You may cancel within 14 days of the start date and receive a full refund, provided no claim has been made. After that you may cancel at any time with seven days' notice; we will refund the premium for the unused period less an administration charge of £25. We may cancel by giving you 30 days' notice in writing to your last known address.

## Renewal

We will write to you at least 21 days before the renewal date with the new premium. If you pay monthly, the policy renews automatically unless you tell us otherwise.
