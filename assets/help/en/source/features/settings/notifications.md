# Notifications

## Common questions

- How do I turn on Do Not Disturb?
- Which notifications still appear during DND?
- Where do I control notification sounds and banners?

## What it is

**Notifications** settings manage how Qube alerts you outside the main window: completion toasts, background job status, and companion attention cues. **Do Not Disturb (critical only)** suppresses non-critical notifications while still allowing urgent messages through.

Use DND during meetings, recordings, or focused work when banner noise would be distracting.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Notifications** (settings section `notifications`). Press **?** for the guided tour (`settings.notifications`).

## Also called

alerts, toast notifications, Do Not Disturb, DND, notification banners, NOTIFICATIONS

## How to…

1. **Review master toggles** — Confirm **Enable notifications** is on before tuning individual categories.
2. **Enable Do Not Disturb** — Toggle **Do Not Disturb (critical only)** when you want minimal interruptions.
3. **Reduce noise while focused** — Enable **Suppress info/success while app is focused** to hide low-priority banners during active use.
4. **Tune categories** — Adjust **OS notifications when hidden**, **Play alert sounds**, **Show message preview in notifications**, and **Memory extraction notifications** to taste.
5. **Clear history** — Use **Clear notification history** to reset the notification centre list.
