## What it is

The **Qube Documentation** collection contains curated guides about using Qube: app pages, settings, workflows, troubleshooting, and **generated reference** pages synced from the application on each release.

## Where to find it

Open **Library** → **Qube**, or use **`@[tool:help]`** in **Conversations**. Reference pages under `reference/` are generated from live registries — do not edit them by hand.
